**A launch film creates interest in a change; a product demo proves how the software works.** They can share footage, but they serve different moments in the buyer journey. If you ask one video to do both jobs, the launch may become a feature list and the demo may never show enough of the product.

## When to use a launch film

Use a launch film when the audience needs to understand **why the product matters now**. It is useful for a company launch, a major product release, a new category position or a funding announcement with a genuine product story behind it.

The film should give the viewer a clear shift: what was difficult before, what becomes possible, and why this team can deliver it. It can show interface moments, but it does not need to teach every click. A founder post, launch page and shorter edits can carry the idea into the feed and beyond.

For an example of a launch campaign, see the [10x case study](/cases). It shows how several films supported one evolving product story.

## When to use a product demo

Use a demo when the viewer already has some interest and asks, “What does it actually do?” A useful demo shows a real workflow, the inputs it needs, the decision it helps make and the result on screen.

Keep the interface legible. One action per visual beat is usually easier to follow than a fast tour of every menu. If different buyer roles need different outcomes, make separate demos or tutorials rather than one broad video.

## A quick decision test

Ask what the audience must be able to say after watching:

- “I understand the new opportunity and want to learn more” points to a launch film.
- “I can see how this workflow would fit my team” points to a demo.
- “I know how to complete the task myself” points to a tutorial.

The answer can be more than one video. A launch film can lead to a demo on the landing page. A demo can lead to a [product education library](/product-education) once a customer signs up.

## Plan the handoff between videos

Choose one claim for the launch film, then decide which product proof the demo should provide. Reuse the same language and visual system so the viewer experiences one story rather than disconnected assets. From the start, decide where each video will live and what action follows it.

The best format is the one that answers the viewer’s current question clearly. Production style follows that decision.
